# First playable build validation

Verified October 1, 2026 on the MacBook Pro. Branch: `feat/playable-rebuild`.

## Results

- `npm test`: 26 tests passed, zero failed. Coverage includes clean starts, all encounter types and choices, exactly-once resolution, pending-event restoration, permanent death, resource affordability, shop returns, profession abilities, consumables, strict save validation, and terminal states.
- `npm run build`: passed. Static output contains HTML, modules, styles, and 29 asset files including their manifest.
- Browser suite: 32 checks passed against the final static build served on localhost. No script errors, broken images, or horizontal overflow were reported.
- Widths: 390, 414, 430, 768, and 1440px. Title, backgrounds, names, shop, and travel were checked at each width. The primary travel action fits in the initial viewport. Selected phone/desktop/ending screenshots were visually inspected.
- Keyboard checks: arrow-key background selection, focus inside native encounter dialogs, Escape preserving unresolved encounters, and focus restoration in interactive flows. Invalid-save preservation and blocked-storage warnings were exercised.
- Full browser win: developer, seed 21, stocked through visible purchase controls and resupplied at stops; reached 1,000 miles on day 16 with five survivors.
- Full browser loss: developer, seed 21, skipped resupply and drove until stranded; ended at mile 350 on day 6 with five survivors. Loss remains terminal even when travelers are alive.
- Both endings survived reload; replay restored a fresh day-1, mile-0 healthy party.
- Rules simulations: stocked seed 21 wins for each of the four backgrounds; the developer stocking policy wins 28 of seeds 1–30. This small deterministic sample establishes route feasibility and regression behavior, not population-level difficulty.

The [browser report](2026-10-01-browser-report.json) records measurements and route outcomes. Reproduction commands are in [Project setup](../../PROJECT_SETUP.md).

## Review fixes

Independent review identified two material issues. Both are fixed, have regression tests, and passed a scoped independent re-review:

1. Good-weather events at a newly arrived stop could bypass a needed shop. At a stop, the event now preserves location and offers 2 fuel; on the road it grants 20 miles.
2. A malformed saved party with no living travelers could remain active and crash during an encounter. Active or won saves now require a living traveler.

## Limits

Browser checks ran in installed desktop Chrome with simulated widths, not on a physical phone or Safari. No site was published. Saves remain browser-local and do not sync. The first version uses static illustrations; scene-to-scene van consistency and decorative generated lettering need a later art pass. Icons and portraits are extracted/resampled concepts, not a validated strict pixel grid. There is no animation or audio. The original humor, including the rare critical pandemic encounter, is retained for user playtesting.
